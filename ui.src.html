CSV format

Needs a key column and at least one of heading, body or ctas. Column order and case don't matter, and extra columns are ignored. Commas, semicolons and tabs are all fine.

keyUnique ID for the entry (required)
headingHeading copy
bodyBody copy
ctasOne or more CTAs separated by |
categoryUsed for the category filter
subcategoryShown on the card, searchable
source_fileGroups entries in the list
